Entry for the make.fun bounty
["Sum up 23 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261123):
one 1024×1024 infographic summing up Monday 23 November 2026 in six
verified stories from around the world.

## The six stories

1. **COP31 collapses** — no deal in Antalya; the final draft drops any
   mention of fossil fuels.
2. **Democrats win 258 House seats** — the largest majority since 1992;
   Hubbard beats Foxx in North Carolina.
3. **KOSPI sinks 3%** — Seoul falls after the U.S. AI sell-off; the
   government readies a stabilization fund.
4. **Factory fire in Indonesia** — 50 missing after a candy factory blaze
   in Cikande, Java; 7 body bags found.
5. **Drone hits flat near Kyiv** — 14 missing in Dymer; a boy, 7, is
   pulled alive after 14 hours under rubble.
6. **Skubal to Mets: $450M** — a record deal for a pitcher, 9 years,
   topping Yamamoto's $325M.

Sources: Reuters · AP · CNN · BBC · Le Monde · Al Jazeera.

## Files

- `main.py` — stdlib-only copier: copies the pre-rendered `entry.png`
  next to it to `/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`).
  No image library, no network, deterministic.
- `entry.png` — the finished 1024×1024 infographic (PNG, ≤ 1 MiB).
- `README.md` — this file.
- `LICENSE` — MIT.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
# writes /output/entry.png
```
